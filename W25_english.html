抽測 A：Level 5 詞彙 15 題
作答說明 限時 10 分鐘，單選題。做完再展開解析；答錯的字，請回到上面的字表打「✘」並抄進錯字本。
1Police must ______ the new traffic rules strictly; otherwise drivers will simply ignore them.
  • (A) testify
  • (B) enforce
  • (C) exploit
  • (D) simulate
答案：(B) enforce
enforce 執行（法規）。testify 作證、exploit 利用、simulate 模擬，都不能搭配 rules。
2Rising sea levels and strong waves have ______ the coastline so badly that several houses had to be moved inland.
  • (A) eroded
  • (B) allocated
  • (C) articulated
  • (D) nourished
答案：(A) eroded
erode 侵蝕。allocate 分配、articulate 清楚表達、nourish 滋養。
3His back pain is ______; it has bothered him for more than ten years and may never disappear completely.
  • (A) immune
  • (B) authentic
  • (C) subtle
  • (D) chronic
答案：(D) chronic
chronic 慢性的、長期的。immune 免疫的、authentic 真正的、subtle 細微的。
4The government decided to ______ more funds to rural schools in order to narrow the education gap.
  • (A) provoke
  • (B) distort
  • (C) allocate
  • (D) fluctuate
答案：(C) allocate
allocate funds 撥款、分配經費。provoke 激怒、distort 扭曲、fluctuate 波動（不及物）。
5The film ______ the life of a poor farmer so honestly that many viewers were moved to tears.
  • (A) prescribed
  • (B) portrayed
  • (C) simulated
  • (D) prosecuted
答案：(B) portrayed
portray 描繪（人物或生活）。prescribe 開處方、simulate 模擬、prosecute 起訴。
6Because he spends most of the day sitting in front of a screen, his doctor warned that his ______ lifestyle might lead to health problems.
  • (A) sedentary
  • (B) versatile
  • (C) reciprocal
  • (D) ethical
答案：(A) sedentary
sedentary lifestyle 久坐不動的生活方式。versatile 多才多藝、reciprocal 互惠的、ethical 倫理的。
7The scientist's ______ that plants grow faster under blue light was later supported by several experiments.
  • (A) glitch
  • (B) tariff
  • (C) verdict
  • (D) hypothesis
答案：(D) hypothesis
hypothesis 假說，後面才會被實驗「支持」。glitch 小故障、tariff 關稅、verdict 裁決。
8Typewriters became ______ once personal computers became cheap and common.
  • (A) therapeutic
  • (B) eloquent
  • (C) obsolete
  • (D) authentic
答案：(C) obsolete
obsolete 過時的、不再使用的。therapeutic 治療的、eloquent 雄辯的、authentic 真品的。
9Rising prices and ______ incomes make it difficult for many families to save any money.
  • (A) streamlined
  • (B) dwindling
  • (C) integrated
  • (D) degraded
答案：(B) dwindling
dwindle 逐漸減少，dwindling incomes 日益減少的收入，與 rising prices 形成對比。
10The two countries signed a ______ promising never to attack each other.
  • (A) treaty
  • (B) genre
  • (C) prototype
  • (D) gradient
答案：(A) treaty
treaty 條約。genre 文類、prototype 原型、gradient 斜率、梯度。
11The company introduced a cash ______ to encourage its employees to cycle to work instead of driving.
  • (A) pollutant
  • (B) paradox
  • (C) algorithm
  • (D) incentive
答案：(D) incentive
incentive 獎勵、誘因，後面 to encourage 為線索。
12It seems a ______ that the richest city in the region has so many homeless people.
  • (A) heritage
  • (B) refugee
  • (C) paradox
  • (D) commodity
答案：(C) paradox
paradox 看似矛盾卻存在的現象。heritage 遺產、refugee 難民、commodity 商品。
13Factories must reduce harmful ______ if the city wants to improve its air quality.
  • (A) censorship
  • (B) emissions
  • (C) methodologies
  • (D) sovereignty
答案：(B) emissions
emission 排放（物）；harmful emissions 有害排放。
14Many people feel ______ about moving abroad: they long for new experiences but fear losing touch with their families.
  • (A) ambivalent
  • (B) renewable
  • (C) underprivileged
  • (D) legitimate
答案：(A) ambivalent
ambivalent 矛盾的、猶豫的；冒號後的兩個對立心情是線索。
15After listening to both sides, the judge announced her ______ that the defendant was not guilty.
  • (A) momentum
  • (B) curriculum
  • (C) diplomat
  • (D) verdict
答案：(D) verdict
verdict 裁決，與 judge、defendant 同屬法律情境。

抽測 B：Level 6 詞彙 15 題
作答說明 限時 10 分鐘，單選題。Level 6 字多為抽象詞，請優先用「句子線索」（因果、轉折、對比）判斷，而不是只靠中文意思。
16The manager's decision seemed completely ______; he rejected the proposal without giving any reason.
  • (A) coherent
  • (B) resilient
  • (C) arbitrary
  • (D) sophisticated
答案：(C) arbitrary
arbitrary 隨意的、武斷的。coherent 連貫的、resilient 有復原力的、sophisticated 精密的。
17Smartphones have become ______ in modern life: people use them on buses, in restaurants, and even in temples.
  • (A) ubiquitous

閱讀 A — Repair Cafés（環境與消費文化）
作答方式 計時 8 分鐘，作答第 1–5 題後再展開解析。畫點線的字為詞彙表中的重點字。

1Since the first one opened in Amsterdam in 2009, repair cafés have quietly spread across the world. On a typical Saturday morning, a community hall fills with people carrying broken toasters, torn jackets and wobbly chairs. Volunteers — retired engineers, tailors and hobbyists — sit beside them, tools in hand, and help them fix the objects free of charge.

5The idea began as a response to throwaway culture. For decades, manufacturers have designed products that are cheaper to replace than to repair; some devices are even built so that their batteries cannot be removed. As a result, millions of tons of electronic waste are produced each year, much of it still functional or easily repairable. Organizers argue that fixing things is not only an environmentally friendly choice, but also a way to rebuild practical skills that modern consumers have largely lost.

11Participants often report benefits that go beyond saving money. Many arrive feeling helpless in front of a broken machine and leave with a sense of achievement after watching, or even helping, to bring it back to life. Strangers swap stories and advice, and older volunteers discover that their experience is finally valued again. "A repaired kettle is nice," one organizer says, "but what really matters is the conversation that happens while we fix it."

16Critics point out that repair cafés cannot solve the waste problem on their own. Without laws that require companies to sell spare parts and publish repair manuals, volunteers are often unable to fix a device, however skilled they are. Some governments have begun to respond: a few countries now give citizens a legal "right to repair," and others offer tax reductions for repair services. Supporters hope that such measures will turn a charming neighborhood activity into a lasting change in how products are made and used.

閱讀 B — The Planning Fallacy（心理學與時間管理）
作答方式 計時 8 分鐘，作答第 1–5 題後再展開解析。這類「現象 → 研究 → 原因 → 對策」的科普結構，是學測閱讀的常見模式。

1Most of us have been there: you estimate that an essay will take two hours, yet it swallows your entire evening. Psychologists call this tendency the planning fallacy — the habit of underestimating how long a task will take, even when we know that similar tasks have run late in the past.

4The idea was first introduced in 1979 by the psychologists Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky. In one well-known study that followed, university students who were writing a thesis were asked to predict when they would finish. On average, they predicted about five weeks; in reality, the job took nearly eight. Fewer than a third completed it within the time they had expected, and even their "worst-case" estimates turned out to be too optimistic.

9Why does this happen? When we plan, we usually imagine the smooth path: no interruptions, no sudden illness, no computer crash. We focus on the details of the task in front of us — what researchers call the "inside view" — and tend to ignore the discrepancy between our past plans and our past results. The remedy is the "outside view": instead of asking, "How long will this take if everything goes well?" we ask, "How long did similar tasks actually take me before?"

15Practical strategies follow naturally. Break a large project into small steps and estimate each one separately; add a time buffer of at least 20 to 30 percent; and keep a simple record of how long each task really takes. Over time, such records become a personal database that is far more reliable than optimism. The goal is not to plan less, but to plan with evidence.

隨堂測驗 8 題（綜合測驗與基礎文法）
綜合測驗（第 1–5 題）：請閱讀下文，選出最適當的選項

Many students feel anxious on Sunday evenings, (1) they have a long week of tests and homework ahead of them. This feeling, sometimes called the "Sunday scaries," is (2) common that psychologists have begun to study it. Researchers suggest that the anxiety often comes from uncertainty rather than from the work itself. When students have no clear plan, small tasks pile up in their minds, (3) them feel overwhelmed. One simple solution is to spend ten minutes on Friday afternoon writing down everything that needs to be done next week. (4) the list is written, the brain no longer has to keep reminding itself, and the weekend feels far (5) relaxing.

1(1)
  • (A) as
  • (B) unless
  • (C) whether
  • (D) although
答案：(A) as
as 在此表原因（因為）；後面 they have a long week ahead 是焦慮的原因。unless 除非、whether 是否、although 雖然，語意皆不通。
2(2)
  • (A) such
  • (B) so
  • (C) very
  • (D) too
答案：(B) so
so + 形容詞 + that…（如此……以致於……）。such 後接名詞；very 後不能接 that 子句；too 需搭配 to。
3(3)
  • (A) make
  • (B) to make
  • (C) making
  • (D) made
答案：(C) making
前面是完整子句，後面用現在分詞表示結果（……使得他們覺得不堪負荷）。make them feel 為使役動詞用法，分詞構句用 making。
4(4)
  • (A) Once
  • (B) Unless
  • (C) Whereas
  • (D) Despite
答案：(A) Once
Once + 子句：一旦……就……。Despite 後只能接名詞；Unless 與語意相反；Whereas 表對比。
5(5)
  • (A) more
  • (B) most
  • (C) much
  • (D) very
答案：(A) more
far more relaxing：far 修飾比較級（far more + 形容詞）。
基礎文法與轉承（第 6–8 題）
6The ______ of the new policy was delayed because there was not enough funding.
  • (A) implement
  • (B) implementation
  • (C) implementing
  • (D) implemented
答案：(B) implementation
the ______ of 後面要接名詞，implement 的名詞形為 implementation。
7It is high time that the government ______ stricter rules on plastic waste.
  • (A) introduce
  • (B) introduces
  • (C) introduced
  • (D) will introduce
答案：(C) introduced
It is (high) time that + 過去式子句，表示「早該……了」，用過去式表示與現實相反的語氣（實際上還沒做）。
8The new bus line cut commuting time by half. ______, the number of cars on the road has dropped sharply.
  • (A) However
  • (B) As a result
  • (C) In contrast
  • (D) Otherwise
答案：(B) As a result
前句是原因（通勤時間縮短一半），後句是結果（車輛減少），所以用 As a result。However 與 In contrast 表轉折或對比；Otherwise 表「否則」。
