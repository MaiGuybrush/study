小試身手：綜合測驗 5 題
Plastic straws may seem harmless, but they __(1)__ a serious threat to sea life. Because they are light, they are easily carried by wind and water into the ocean, __(2)__ they can be swallowed by turtles and seabirds. In response, many cities have banned single-use straws. __(3)__, some people argue that the bans place a burden on people with disabilities, who may rely on bendable straws to drink. A better solution, they suggest, is to offer straws only __(4)__ request. __(5)__ we choose, reducing plastic waste will require both policy and personal effort.
1(1)
  • (A) make
  • (B) pose
  • (C) take
  • (D) do
答案：(B) pose
pose a threat to 對…構成威脅，固定搭配。
2(2)
  • (A) which
  • (B) that
  • (C) where
  • (D) when
答案：(C) where
先行詞為 the ocean（地點），且關係子句「they can be swallowed…」結構完整，需關係副詞 where；逗號後不可用 that。
3(3)
  • (A) Therefore
  • (B) For example
  • (C) However
  • (D) As a result
答案：(C) However
前句說許多城市禁用吸管，後句提出反對意見，為轉折關係。
4(4)
  • (A) in
  • (B) on
  • (C) by
  • (D) at
答案：(B) on
on request「應要求（才提供）」，固定介係詞片語。
5(5)
  • (A) Whatever
  • (B) Whether
  • (C) However
  • (D) What
答案：(A) Whatever
Whatever we choose = No matter what we choose「無論我們選擇什麼」，choose 缺受詞，需用 whatever。Whether 後須接完整子句並常搭配 or。
三、文意選填（10 題｜10 分）
詞性分類法（四步驟） ① 先把 10 個選項依詞性分組（n. / v. / adj. / adv.），並注意動詞時態、名詞單複數
② 在每個空格旁標出需要的詞性（冠詞後 → n.；be 動詞後 → adj. 或 p.p.；主詞後 → v.）
③ 先做只有一個候選字的空格，逐步縮小範圍
④ 最後 2–3 格回頭整段重讀，確認語意通順
陷阱提醒 文意選填 10 格配 10 個選項，選錯一個常會連鎖錯兩個。若最後剩兩格互相卡住，代表前面某一格選錯了，回頭檢查語意最勉強的那一格。
小試身手：5 格配 7 個選項
Bubble tea was first created in Taiwan in the 1980s. What began as a simple tea drink with chewy tapioca balls has since become a global __(1)__. Today, shops selling the drink can be found in cities from London to Los Angeles. Its __(2)__ is often explained by the fact that customers can __(3)__ almost every part of the drink, from the sweetness level to the amount of ice. However, health experts warn that a large cup may contain a __(4)__ amount of sugar. They suggest that drinkers choose less sugar and treat bubble tea as an __(5)__ treat rather than a daily habit.
(A) popularity(B) customize(C) phenomenon(D) surprising(E) occasional(F) customized(G) popular
1(1)–(5) 的答案依序為？
答案：(1) C　(2) A　(3) B　(4) D　(5) E
(1) a global + 名詞 → phenomenon（現象）；(2) Its + 名詞 → popularity；(3) can + 原形動詞 → customize（F 為過去分詞，不可接在 can 後）；(4) a + ___ + amount → 形容詞 surprising；(5) an + 母音開頭形容詞 → occasional。G popular 與 F customized 為干擾選項。
四、篇章結構（4 題｜8 分）
四大線索 ① 代名詞／指示詞：this、these、such、they 必須指得到前文的名詞
② 轉承詞：選項句首的 However / For example / As a result 必須與前句邏輯相符
③ 重複關鍵字：選項中的名詞常在空格前後句再次出現
④ 時序與列舉：First / Then / Finally；one…another…the other
作答順序 先讀每個選項並圈出開頭的轉承詞與代名詞 → 再讀文章，遇到空格就比對線索 → 最後剩下的那一句（5 選 4）往往是「看似相關但缺乏銜接」的干擾句。
小試身手：3 格配 4 個選項
Honeybees communicate in a remarkable way. When a worker bee finds a good source of food, she returns to the hive and performs a "waggle dance." __(1)__ The angle of the dance shows the direction of the food relative to the sun, while the length of the waggle shows how far away it is. __(2)__ Scientists have even used this information to map where bees search for food across the seasons. __(3)__ Without their dances, the colony would waste much more energy looking for flowers.
(A) This dance gives other bees surprisingly precise information.
(B) By watching carefully, other workers can fly almost directly to the food.
(C) Honey has been used as a medicine for thousands of years.
(D) Such efficiency is one reason bee colonies can survive in harsh environments.
1(1)–(3) 的答案依序為？
答案：(1) A　(2) B　(3) D
(1) This dance 承接前句的 waggle dance；(2) 前句說明舞蹈傳達方向與距離，接著寫其他工蜂據此飛向食物；(3) Such efficiency 總結前文，且下一句 Without their dances… 繼續談效率。C 講蜂蜜藥用，離題，為干擾句。
五、閱讀測驗（約 12 題｜24 分）
六大題型與作答技巧
題型常見問法技巧
主旨題What is the passage mainly about? / Which is the best title?看首段末句與各段首句；刪去「太廣」或「只講一段」的選項
細節題According to the passage, which is true?回原文定位關鍵字，注意選項的「同義改寫」
推論題What can be inferred from…?答案「文中沒直接說但必然成立」；避免過度延伸
字義題The word "___" in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to…把選項代回原句；看前後句的解釋或對比
指涉題What does "it / this / they" refer to?往前找最近、且單複數相符的名詞
態度／目的題What is the author's attitude toward…? / Why does the author mention…?留意評價性形容詞與副詞（fortunately, questionable）
常見錯誤選項的四種樣貌 ① 原文的字都有，但因果關係顛倒｜② 用 always / never / all 等絕對字眼｜③ 符合常識但文中沒提｜④ 只對了一半（前半正確、後半錯）
小試身手：限時 6 分鐘

For decades, scientists believed that sleep was simply a period of rest, a time when the brain switched off to recover from the day. Recent research, however, paints a very different picture. While we sleep, the brain is surprisingly busy, sorting through the experiences of the day and deciding what to keep.

During deep sleep, the hippocampus, a region that temporarily stores new information, "replays" the day's events. These signals are then sent to the cortex, where memories can be stored for the long term. In one common type of experiment, volunteers learn a list of word pairs in the evening. Those who sleep a full night usually remember more pairs the next morning than those who stay awake for the same length of time.

Sleep does not only help us remember; it also helps us forget. Researchers have found evidence that connections between brain cells that are rarely used become weaker during sleep. This "clean-up" process may leave room for new learning the following day.

These findings carry a clear message for students. Staying up late to cram before an exam may feel productive, but cutting sleep can undo much of the hard work. A regular sleep schedule, it seems, is one of the most effective study tools available.

1What is the passage mainly about?
  • (A) Why students should study at night
  • (B) The active role sleep plays in processing memories
  • (C) How the hippocampus was discovered
  • (D) The dangers of sleeping too much
答案：(B)
首段指出「睡眠不只是休息」，其後各段說明睡眠如何保存與清理記憶。C 文中未提；D 與內容相反；A 與末段建議相反。
2According to the passage, what does the hippocampus do?
  • (A) It stores memories permanently.
  • (B) It shuts down during deep sleep.
  • (C) It holds new information for a short time before passing it on.
  • (D) It removes unused connections between brain cells.
答案：(C)
原文 "a region that temporarily stores new information"，再傳送到 cortex 長期保存。A 把 cortex 的功能錯置；D 文中未說是 hippocampus 執行。
3The word "cram" in the last paragraph is closest in meaning to ______.
  • (A) study intensively in a short time
  • (B) relax before a test
  • (C) cheat on an exam
  • (D) review old notes slowly
答案：(A)
Staying up late to ___ before an exam「考前熬夜臨時抱佛腳」，前後文 may feel productive 也提示是「努力讀書」的行為。
4What can be inferred from the passage?
  • (A) Forgetting is always harmful to learning.
  • (B) Students who cut sleep before a test may remember less of what they studied.
  • (C) Deep sleep is the only stage in which the brain is active.
  • (D) Word-pair experiments prove that sleep cures memory loss.
答案：(B)
由 "cutting sleep can undo much of the hard work" 可推得。A 與第三段相反（遺忘有助騰出空間）；C、D 使用 only / cures 等過度延伸的說法。
六、混合題（約 4 題｜10 分）
作答規則（最容易因「形式」被扣分） ① 依題目指定：「從文中選出一個單詞」就只能寫一個字，且必須是文中原字
② 題目要求改寫詞性時（如 "in its correct form"），注意單複數、時態、詞性變化
③ 拼字錯誤不給分；專有名詞注意大小寫

小試身手
The Pomodoro Technique was developed in the late 1980s by Francesco Cirillo, then a university student. Named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer he used, the method breaks work into short, focused periods. First, choose a single task. Next, set a timer for 25 minutes and work until it rings. Then take a five-minute break. After four such work periods, take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. Supporters say the method reduces distraction because each work period feels short enough to manage, while critics argue that fixed intervals can interrupt deep concentration on complex problems.
StepAction
1Choose a single ____(A)____.
2Work for ____(B)____ minutes.
3Take a short break.
4After ____(C)____ work periods, take a longer break.
1填表：(A)(B)(C) 各填入一個英文單詞或數字。
答案：(A) task　(B) 25　(C) four
直接擷取原文；(A) 不可寫成 tasks（原文為 a single task）。
2簡答：According to critics, what is one weakness of the method? (Answer in no more than 12 words.)
參考答案：Fixed intervals can interrupt deep concentration on complex problems.（9 字）
抓 critics argue that 之後的內容，改寫成完整句並控制字數。

練習 4 句
1近年來，越來越多年輕人選擇利用網路學習新的技能。
In recent years, more and more young people have chosen to learn new skills online.
「近年來」→ 現在完成式 have chosen；online 亦可寫作 through the Internet。
2如果我們不減少塑膠的使用，海洋生物將持續受到威脅。
If we do not reduce the use of plastic, marine life will continue to be threatened.
條件句 if 子句用現在式表未來；「受到威脅」用被動 to be threatened。
3直到考試結束，他才明白充足睡眠的重要性。
It was not until the exam was over that he realized the importance of getting enough sleep.
亦可倒裝：Not until the exam was over did he realize the importance of getting enough sleep.
4這座城市以美食聞名，每年吸引數百萬名觀光客。
The city is famous for its food, attracting millions of tourists every year.
第二個動作以分詞構句 attracting 表附帶結果；「數百萬」為 millions of，不可寫 millions tourists。

隨堂測驗 10 題（全題型綜合）
詞彙題（第 1–3 題）
1Despite the heavy rain, the marathon went ahead as ______, and thousands of runners showed up.
  • (A) scheduled
  • (B) canceled
  • (C) delayed
  • (D) excluded
答案：(A) scheduled
Despite 表讓步：雖然下大雨，馬拉松仍「如期」舉行（as scheduled）。
2The new policy aims to ______ the gap between urban and rural schools by sending more resources to remote areas.
  • (A) widen
  • (B) bridge
  • (C) block
  • (D) ignore
答案：(B) bridge
bridge the gap「縮小差距」，高頻搭配詞；by sending more resources 為手段線索。
3Many people find it hard to stay ______ when they are criticized in public.
  • (A) composed
  • (B) exposed
  • (C) opposed
  • (D) imposed
答案：(A) composed
composed 鎮定的；stay composed「保持冷靜」。其餘為形近字干擾。
文法與轉承（第 4–8 題）
4______ from the top of the mountain, the city looks like a glittering sea of lights.
  • (A) Seeing
  • (B) Seen
  • (C) To see
  • (D) Having seen
答案：(B) Seen
主詞 the city 是「被看」，分詞構句用過去分詞 Seen。
5Not until the power came back ______ how much we depend on electricity.
  • (A) we realized
  • (B) did we realize
  • (C) we did realize
  • (D) realized we
答案：(B) did we realize
Not until 置於句首，主要子句須倒裝（助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞）。
6If I ______ harder last year, I would be studying at my dream university now.
  • (A) studied
  • (B) had studied
  • (C) have studied
  • (D) would study
答案：(B) had studied
混合假設：條件與「過去」事實相反（last year）→ had p.p.；結果與「現在」事實相反（now）→ would be。
7The new phone has many impressive features. ______, its high price may keep many students from buying it.
  • (A) Therefore
  • (B) Moreover
  • (C) However
  • (D) For instance
答案：(C) However
前句優點、後句缺點，轉折關係。
8Regular exercise improves physical health. ______, it can reduce stress and improve mood.
  • (A) In addition
  • (B) Otherwise
  • (C) Nevertheless
  • (D) In contrast
答案：(A) In addition
兩句皆為運動的好處，追加關係。
閱讀字義與中譯英（第 9–10 題）
9"The museum's collection is so vast that visitors could spend days there without seeing everything." The word vast means ______.
  • (A) huge
  • (B) valuable
  • (C) ancient
  • (D) crowded
答案：(A) huge
so…that 後的結果「花好幾天也看不完」，推知 vast 表示規模巨大。
10「他花了三個小時才完成這份報告。」It ______ him three hours to finish the report.
  • (A) spent
  • (B) took
  • (C) cost
  • (D) paid
答案：(B) took
It takes + 人 + 時間 + to V。spend 的主詞須為人（He spent three hours finishing the report.）；cost 多用於金錢或代價。
