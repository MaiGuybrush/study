主題 A：現象＋原因影響（科技與學習）
提示（本講義依 115 年題型自擬）：近年來，許多學生使用 AI 聊天機器人協助學習。請以此為主題，寫一篇英文作文，文長至少 120 個單詞，文分兩段。第一段描述學生如何使用這類工具；第二段根據你的經驗或觀察，說明此現象的原因以及可能的影響。
請先自己寫限時 25 分鐘寫完再展開範文。寫好後用上方自評表打分。
大綱範例
段落主題句細節
第一段AI 聊天機器人已成為學生日常的一部分在哪裡用（公車、圖書館、深夜）；用來做什麼（解數學、改文法）；班上幾乎人人用過
第二段主因是方便，但方便帶來風險原因：隨時可問、害羞的學生敢問。影響：同學抄到錯答案被扣分（具體例子）；思考能力退化。結尾：當夥伴，不當替代品

Over the past few years, AI chatbots have quietly become part of students' daily routines. On the bus, in the library, or at the kitchen table late at night, teenagers type questions into their phones and receive answers within seconds. Some ask the programs to explain difficult math problems, while others use them to check the grammar in their English compositions. In my class, it is now unusual to find anyone who has never tried one.

In my opinion, the main reason for this trend is convenience. Unlike a teacher or a tutor, a chatbot is available at any hour and never gets tired of repeated questions, so shy students feel comfortable asking for help. However, this convenience comes with risks. Last semester, a classmate of mine copied an answer that sounded plausible but was completely wrong, and he lost points on a quiz. If students stop thinking for themselves, their cognitive skills may deteriorate over time. Therefore, I believe AI should serve as a study partner rather than a substitute for our own effort.

為什麼這篇能拿高分（全文 175 字）
① 兩段任務完全對應提示：第一段「如何使用」，第二段「原因」＋「影響」。② 第一段用三個地點、兩種用途，描述很具體。③ 第二段有親身觀察的例子（同學抄錯答案），避免空泛。④ 句型多變：while、unlike、關係子句 that sounded…、if 條件句、rather than。⑤ 橘底是轉承語，藍字是 W25 的 Level 5 字。
主題 B：經驗＋反思（教育與社會）
提示（本講義依 112、114 年題型自擬）：每個人都可能對某些人或事抱持刻板印象，後來才發現並非如此。請以此為主題，寫一篇英文作文，文長至少 120 個單詞，文分兩段。第一段描述一次讓你改變想法的經驗；第二段說明你的想法如何改變，以及這次經驗對你的影響。
請先自己寫敘事題最常見的問題是時態跳動：第一段用過去式，第二段的「現在的想法」才用現在式／現在完成式。
大綱範例
段落主題句細節
第一段我以前認為長者學不會新科技，當志工後改觀時間地點：高一週六、社區中心；人物：78 歲退休護理師林太太；轉折：一小時學會視訊，還問網路詐騙
第二段偏見會遮蔽我們對他人能力的認識想法改變：她只是缺少機會；行動改變：先聽再判斷、每月持續當志工；收尾：一堂最寶貴的課

When I was in my first year of senior high school, I believed that older people could not understand modern technology. That stereotype changed one Saturday when I volunteered at a community center near my home. My job was to teach senior citizens how to use smartphones, and I was paired with Mrs. Lin, a seventy-eight-year-old retired nurse. To my surprise, she not only learned how to make video calls within an hour but also asked me sharp questions about online scams. By the end of the day, she was showing other learners how to send photos to their grandchildren.

This experience made me realize how easily prejudice can blind us to other people's abilities. Mrs. Lin did not lack intelligence; she simply had not been given the chance to practice. Since then, I have tried to listen before I judge, whether I am talking with my grandparents or with classmates I barely know. I have also kept volunteering at the center once a month, because helping others has empowered me as much as it has empowered them. What I thought was a simple Saturday job turned out to be one of the most valuable lessons of my high school life.

為什麼這篇能拿高分（全文 201 字）
① 時態清楚：第一段過去式敘事；第二段 Since then 接現在完成式。② 細節具體到人物年齡、職業與動作。③ 主旨句 how easily prejudice can blind us 是全文的反思核心。④ 善用 not only…but also、分號、whether…or…、What I thought was… 等句型。⑤ 結尾呼應開頭的 Saturday，有收束感。

中譯英五步法與扣分地雷
步驟做什麼
① 找主幹先抓「誰＋做什麼」，把修飾語先放一邊。
② 定時態看時間線索：「現在已經／近年來」→ 現在完成式；「過去」→ 過去式；「如果當時」→ 假設語氣。
③ 選句型中文有「不只……也」「越……越」「直到……才」就對應固定句構。
④ 填詞彙想不到的字用簡單字代換（不知道 utensils 就寫 spoons and chopsticks），別空白。
⑤ 檢查主動詞一致、冠詞、單複數、介系詞、句首大寫與句尾句點。
最常見的扣分地雷 ① 一句兩個動詞沒連接（People use phones check news.）② 動名詞主詞用複數動詞（Getting up early are… → is）③ 不可數名詞加 s（informations, advices, equipments）④ 中文「讓」直譯（let him happy → make him happy）⑤ although 與 but 並用、because 與 so 並用 ⑥ 忘了句首大寫或句尾句點（官方明列扣 0.5 分）。
Day 1（一）環境｜現在完成式、不定詞表目的
1近年來，越來越多人已經開始自備餐具，以減少一次性塑膠的使用。
提示：自備 bring one's own；餐具 utensils；一次性 single-use；「近年來」→ 現在完成式
In recent years, more and more people have started bringing their own utensils to reduce the use of single-use plastics.
「more and more＋複數名詞」後接複數動詞 have started。
2氣候變遷不只影響環境，也改變了我們的生活方式。
提示：氣候變遷 climate change；not only…but also…
Climate change not only affects the environment but also changes the way we live.
the way we live 中不可再加 how。
3如果我們現在不採取行動，下一代將付出更高的代價。
提示：採取行動 take action；付出代價 pay a price；下一代 the next generation
If we do not take action now, the next generation will pay a higher price.
if 子句用現在式代替未來，主要子句才用 will。
Day 2（二）科技｜關係子句、使役句
4許多學生使用人工智慧工具來整理筆記，但他們仍須自行判斷資訊是否正確。
提示：人工智慧 artificial intelligence；判斷 judge；是否 whether
Many students use artificial intelligence tools to organize their notes, but they still have to judge for themselves whether the information is correct.
information 不可數，動詞用 is。
5智慧型手機讓我們能隨時取得資訊，卻也使我們更難專心。
提示：allow sb. to V；隨時 at any time；make it harder for sb. to V
Smartphones allow us to access information at any time, but they also make it harder for us to concentrate.
make it＋形容詞＋for sb. to V，it 不可省。
6這款由臺灣學生設計的應用程式，已經幫助數千名長者學會使用網路。
提示：應用程式 app；由…設計 designed by；長者 senior citizens；數千 thousands of
This app, which was designed by Taiwanese students, has helped thousands of senior citizens learn to use the Internet.
主詞 This app 單數，動詞用 has helped；Internet 大寫。
Day 3（三）健康｜動名詞當主詞、suggest 句型
7每天睡足七到八小時，對青少年的身心健康至關重要。
提示：be essential to；身心健康 physical and mental health
Getting seven to eight hours of sleep every day is essential to teenagers' physical and mental health.
動名詞當主詞視為單數，用 is。
8醫生建議我們少喝含糖飲料，多吃蔬菜水果。
提示：suggest that＋S＋(should) V；含糖飲料 sugary drinks
Doctors suggest that we (should) drink fewer sugary drinks and eat more fruits and vegetables.
suggest 後的 that 子句用原形動詞；不寫 suggest us to。
9長時間盯著螢幕不但傷害視力，也可能導致睡眠品質下降。
提示：盯著 stare at；視力 eyesight；導致 lead to
Staring at screens for long periods of time not only harms our eyesight but may also lead to poor sleep quality.
lead to 的 to 是介系詞，後接名詞。
Day 4（四）教育｜the more…、so…that…、instead of
10我們讀得越多，就越能理解不同的文化與觀點。
提示：The＋比較級…, the＋比較級…；觀點 perspectives
The more we read, the better we can understand different cultures and perspectives.
兩邊的 the 都不可省。
11許多高中生課業壓力太大，以致沒有時間培養興趣。
提示：be under pressure；so much…that…；培養興趣 develop interests
Many senior high school students are under so much academic pressure that they have no time to develop their interests.
pressure 不可數，用 so much，不用 so many。
12老師鼓勵學生勇於提問，而不是只背誦課本上的答案。
提示：encourage sb. to V；instead of＋V-ing；背誦 memorize
Teachers encourage students to ask questions bravely instead of just memorizing the answers in textbooks.
instead of 是介系詞片語，後接 V-ing。
Day 5（五）文化｜分詞構句、Not until 倒裝
13走在老街上，遊客可以品嚐傳統小吃並欣賞古老建築。
提示：分詞構句 V-ing；小吃 snacks；欣賞 admire
Walking along the old street, tourists can taste traditional snacks and admire ancient buildings.
分詞構句的主詞要與主要子句相同（遊客走路）。
14直到出國留學，他才了解自己的文化有多麼珍貴。
提示：Not until＋子句＋倒裝；珍貴 precious
Not until he studied abroad did he realize how precious his own culture was.
Not until 放句首，主要子句要倒裝（did he realize）。也可寫 He did not realize … until he studied abroad.
15每年都有成千上萬的人來到臺灣，體驗這裡的節慶與美食。
提示：thousands of；體驗 experience；節慶 festivals
Every year, thousands of people come to Taiwan to experience its festivals and delicious food.
thousands of 後接複數名詞；不寫 thousand of。
Day 6（六）歷史｜與過去事實相反的假設、分詞片語
16歷史告訴我們，任何重大的改變都需要時間和耐心。
提示：重大的 major；take time and patience
History tells us that any major change takes time and patience.
any＋單數名詞，動詞用 takes；history、patience 不可數。
17如果當時政府早一點採取行動，這場災難或許就能避免。
提示：If＋had p.p., S＋might have p.p.；避免 avoid
If the government had taken action earlier, this disaster might have been avoided.
與過去事實相反用過去完成式；災難被避免要用被動。
18這座博物館保存了許多珍貴的文物，使我們得以一窺過去的生活。
提示：保存 preserve；文物 artifacts；一窺 catch a glimpse of
This museum preserves many precious artifacts, allowing us to catch a glimpse of life in the past.
逗號後用分詞 allowing 表結果，不可接 allows。
Day 7（日）社會與旅遊｜no matter、with 片語
19無論結果如何，重要的是我們從失敗中學到了什麼。
提示：No matter what；what matters is…；失敗 failure
No matter what the result is, what matters is what we have learned from failure.
no matter what 的子句用直述語序（the result is）。
20隨著高齡化社會來臨，照顧長者已成為每個家庭必須面對的問題。
提示：with the arrival of；高齡化社會 an aging society；the elderly
With the arrival of an aging society, taking care of the elderly has become a problem every family must face.
the elderly 指長者全體，視為複數，不加 s；動名詞主詞用 has。
21旅行不只讓我們看見世界，也讓我們更了解自己。
提示：not only…but also…；help sb. V
Traveling not only lets us see the world but also helps us understand ourselves better.
let、help 後接原形動詞。
近五年中譯英真題 10 句（大考中心）
作答方式每題限時 2 分鐘，自己寫完再展開官方滿分參考答案。答案可有多種寫法，官方均列出同義替換，這裡節錄一種，可替換的字列在括號中。
1115-1　現在越來越多高中英文老師已經增加在課堂上使用英文的百分比。
Now, more and more senior high school English teachers have increased the percentage of English use in class.
可替換：Now（Nowadays）；English use（using English）
2115-2　他們將學生依英語能力分成不同組別，進行多樣的聽、說活動。
They divide students into different groups based on their English ability and conduct various listening and speaking activities.
可替換：ability（proficiency）；various（diverse, a variety of）
3114-1　人類的想像和創意是科技進步最大的驅動力。
Human imagination and creativity is the biggest driving force for technological advancement.
可替換：biggest（strongest）；force（power）；advancement（advances, advance）
4114-2　過去在科幻電影中出現的神奇物件，現在正逐一成真。
Amazing objects that appeared in science-fiction movies in the past are now becoming reality one by one.
可替換：Amazing（Fantastic）；objects（things）；movies（films）；reality（real）
5113-1　每逢選舉季節，總會看到政治人物造訪各地著名廟宇。
During election season, politicians can be seen visiting famous temples in different places.
可替換：During（Every）；can（will）；visiting（making visits to）；places（areas）
6113-2　除了祈求好的選舉結果，他們也希望展現對在地文化與習俗的尊重。
Besides praying for good election results, they also hope to show respect to local culture and customs.

7112-1　歷史一再證明，戰爭會造成極為可怕的災難。
History has proved repeatedly that wars will cause extremely terrible disasters.
可替換：has proved（proves, has proven）；repeatedly（time and again）；cause（lead to）；terrible（horrible）
8112-2　避免衝突、確保世界和平應該是所有人類追求的目標。
To avoid conflicts and ensure world peace should be the goal pursued by all human beings.
可替換：To avoid…ensure（Avoiding…ensuring）
9111-1　飼養寵物並非一項短暫的人生體驗，而是一個對動物的終生承諾。
Keeping a pet is not a short-term life experience, but a lifelong commitment to an animal.
可替換：Keeping（Raising）；short-term life experience（temporary experience in life）；commitment（promise）
10111-2　在享受寵物所帶來的歡樂時，我們不該忽略要善盡照顧他們的責任。
When enjoying the joy pets bring (to) us, we should not ignore the responsibility of taking good care of them.
可替換：joy（fun, pleasure）；ignore（neglect, overlook）

隨堂測驗 8 題：找出錯誤並訂正
作答說明每句有一處主要錯誤，都是常見的失分點。先自己訂正，再展開解答。
1Using smartphones before bed make it hard for many teenagers to fall asleep.
make → makes
動名詞片語當主詞視為單數。
2Doctors suggest us to exercise for thirty minutes every day.
suggest us to exercise → suggest that we (should) exercise
suggest 不接「人＋不定詞」。
3Many students have no time to develop their interests because of too many pressure.
too many pressure → too much pressure
pressure 不可數，用 much。
4I have volunteered at the community center last summer.
have volunteered → volunteered
有明確過去時間（last summer）用過去式。
5Although it rained heavily, but we still finished the beach cleanup.
刪除 but
although 與 but 不可並用。
6Walking along the old street, the traditional snacks attracted me.
the traditional snacks attracted me → I was attracted by the traditional snacks
分詞構句主詞須與主要子句相同（走路的是「我」）。
7The teacher gave us many useful informations about the exam.
many useful informations → a lot of useful information
information 不可數。
8Not until he went abroad he realized how precious his culture was.
he realized → did he realize
Not until 放句首，主要子句要倒裝。
